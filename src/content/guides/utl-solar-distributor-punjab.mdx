Every product page lists UTL's specifications in full, including ratings, voltages, dimensions, weights and warranty. If a figure matters for your design, we confirm it against the current UTL datasheet before you order.

## Which UTL product for which job

- For a grid-connected home or business, use UTL solar panels with an on-grid inverter. That is the system that gets the PM Surya Ghar subsidy for homes.
- If power cuts are the problem, a hybrid PCU with batteries runs your essentials while the grid is down. See [hybrid solar systems](/hybrid-solar-systems/).
- A farmhouse or site with no grid supply needs a matched off-grid kit. See [off-grid solar systems](/off-grid-solar-systems/).
- Already own an inverter? A UTL charge controller or solar management unit can add panels to it.
- For e-rickshaws and e-bikes there are UTL lithium batteries and EV battery chargers.

Not sure which size you need? The [solar calculator](/solar-calculator/) works it out from your PSPCL bill, and the [new-house calculator](/new-house-solar-calculator/) from your appliances.
