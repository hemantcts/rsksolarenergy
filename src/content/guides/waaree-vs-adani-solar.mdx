## Choose Waaree if

- You want the product warranty in writing on the exact model, which Waaree states at 12 years here.
- You do not need the subsidy, or you are quoted a different Waaree model that is DCR.

## Choose Adani if

- You want the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and the convenience of a DCR version of the same series.

## What neither headline tells you

DCR panels use Indian-made cells, and only DCR panels qualify for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/). They cost noticeably more than non-DCR panels, so a cheap quote on a non-DCR model can cost you the subsidy. Read [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/) before comparing prices.
