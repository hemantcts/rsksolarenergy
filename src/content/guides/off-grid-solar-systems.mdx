## How an off-grid system works

<SystemFlow type="off-grid" />

Panels charge a battery bank through a charge controller, and an inverter turns the battery power into normal 230 V AC for your loads. With no connection to PSPCL, nothing is exported and there is no electricity bill for the circuits the system runs. That also means it has to be sized to make and store all the energy you use.

## Sizing: energy first, then storage

1. List the loads and the hours each one runs per day, then multiply watts by hours to get units (kWh).
2. Size the panels to make that many units on a typical day, with some margin for winter and cloudy spells.
3. Size the batteries for the hours without sun, without draining them too deeply, because deep discharge shortens battery life.

The [off-grid calculator](/hybrid-off-grid-solar-calculator/?mode=off-grid) does these three steps for the appliances you choose, and shows the roof area and a price range.

Off-grid systems most often go wrong at this stage, with panels sized for summer and batteries too small for a winter evening. Size the system for the worst month of the year.

## Upkeep

Keep the panels clean, especially in dusty months, and check the batteries. Tubular batteries need their water topped up, and the float indicators on UTL tubular batteries show when. Plan for a battery replacement during the life of the panels.
