## How an off-grid system works

Panels charge a battery bank through a charge controller. An inverter turns the battery power into normal 230 V AC for your loads. There is no connection to PSPCL, so nothing is exported and there is no electricity bill for the circuits it runs. The system has to be sized to make and store all the energy you use.

## Sizing: energy first, then storage

1. **List the loads** and the hours each runs per day. Multiply watts by hours to get units (kWh).
2. **Size the panels** to make that many units on a typical day, with margin for winter and cloudy spells.
3. **Size the batteries** for the hours without sun, without draining them too deep. Deep discharge shortens battery life.

This is where off-grid systems go wrong most often: panels sized for summer and batteries too small for a winter evening. Size for the worst month, not the best.

## Upkeep

Keep the panels clean, especially in dusty months, and check the batteries. Tubular batteries need their water level topped up; the float indicators on UTL tubular batteries show when. Plan for battery replacement during the life of the panels.
