## Choose Rayzon if

- A longer product warranty is what you weigh most. Rayzon lists 15 years on materials.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the panels with the inverter and battery from one brand, priced as one system.
- You need DCR panels for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/). UTL makes DCR TOPCon modules; see [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/) for why that matters.
