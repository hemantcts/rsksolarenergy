## The short answer

Choose N-type TOPCon, which has the highest efficiency of the common types and copes best with heat. Most TOPCon panels are now half-cut and bifacial as well. If you are claiming the subsidy, the panels must also be DCR and on the ALMM list.

The last two are not rival technologies. A single panel can be TOPCon, half-cut and bifacial all at once, and many are.

## When the answer is different

- A solar water pump or a small off-grid system with plenty of space: mono PERC can do the job for less. See [solar pumps](/solar-pumps/) and [off-grid systems](/off-grid-solar-systems/).
- A business, which the home subsidy does not cover: non-DCR panels are cheaper for the same output. See [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).
- A flat, dark roof with no room for a raised frame: bifacial adds little, so do not pay extra for it alone. See [bifacial panels](/bifacial-solar-panels/).
