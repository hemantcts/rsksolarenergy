UTL's longest-warranty battery sits outside this table because it is larger: the [170 Ah USB 17100](/products/utl-170ah-inverter-battery-usb-17100/) lists 100 months in total, though UTL does not state how that divides between free replacement and pro-rata.

## Choose Livguard if

- You want the longest battery warranty on a 150 Ah model. The IT1584TT lists 60 + 24 months.

## Choose UTL if

- You are comparing like with like at 36 + 24 months, where the two match, and want the battery to come from the same brand as your inverter and panels.
- You want one service network for the whole system. That matters most on a [hybrid system](/hybrid-solar-systems/), where the battery and the inverter work together.
