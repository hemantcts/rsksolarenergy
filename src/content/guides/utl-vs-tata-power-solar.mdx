On panel warranties UTL is level or ahead: Tata Power's Solaroof page lists a 25-year module warranty, while UTL's 545 W TOPCon panel lists a 30-year performance warranty. Tata's strengths are scale and a single responsible company, with a 5-year warranty on grid-connected systems and a network it puts at 575 channel partners and 225 service partners. With UTL, the warranties are UTL's own, product by product.

## Choose Tata Power Solar if

- **You want one national company responsible** for the whole installed system.
- **A Tata partner is closer to you** than a UTL distributor.

## Choose UTL through RSK if

- **You want a local team to design and install your system,** with an office you can visit in Mohali.
- **You want to pick on-grid, hybrid or off-grid from one catalogue,** including the lithium hybrid kits we quote from our own itemised prices.
- **You are in Tricity or around Kurali, Morinda and Ropar,** where RSK's own team installs.
