UTL's clearest advantage is range. It sells every part of a home solar system under one brand: panels, on-grid string inverters from 1.5 kW to 125 kW, hybrid and off-grid PCUs, tubular and lithium batteries, and charge controllers. On panel warranties it is level with the big panel makers. On inverters and batteries it is level with Luminous and Microtek overall, ahead on some models and behind on others.
