Adani's Shine TOPCon series (610 to 640 Wp) lists up to 22.3% efficiency, a 30-year power warranty and 0.40% degradation a year, in DCR and non-DCR versions. UTL's 630 Wp G12R TOPCon panel lists 23.32%, and its 545 W TOPCon panel 22.74% with a 30-year performance warranty. The bigger difference is what else each company sells. Adani Solar's product page lists PV modules only, while UTL supplies the inverter, battery and charge controller as well.

## Why a one-brand system can matter

When panels, inverter and battery come from one brand, the brand's own kits match the voltages and currents, and one warranty process covers the lot. With Adani panels, the inverter and battery come from someone else, which works well as long as the installer matches them properly.

## Choose Adani Solar if

- You only need panels, and you already have an inverter and battery you are happy with.
- A 610 to 640 Wp module suits your roof layout.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the whole system from one brand, matched in UTL's own kits.
- You want DCR TOPCon panels for a subsidised home system, alongside a UTL inverter.
