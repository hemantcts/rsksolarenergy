## Two faces, one panel

A normal panel has a solid, opaque back sheet. A bifacial panel has glass or a clear sheet on the back as well, and cells that respond to light from either side. The front still does most of the work. The back picks up what reflects from beneath: off the roof, the ground, or a wall.

Most bifacial panels are also dual-glass, glass on both faces, which makes them stiffer and better sealed against moisture than a panel with a plastic back sheet.

## What decides how much the back adds

- The surface underneath. White or light-coloured roofs and pale stone reflect far more than dark ones.
- The height. A panel on a raised frame lets reflected light reach the whole of its back; a panel mounted flat and low shades its own rear.
- The spacing. Rows packed tightly shade the ground under each other.

No single figure fits every roof, which is why this page does not quote one. Ask for the datasheet's bifaciality figure, and treat any promise of a fixed extra percentage with care.

## Where it pays in Punjab

Many homes here put panels on a raised steel frame so the roof stays usable for drying clothes or storage. That is close to ideal for bifacial panels: height, and usually a light roof. You can see the shape of it in the [installations we have photographed](/installations/). Ground-mounted arrays over pale soil, such as those for [solar water pumps](/solar-pumps/), are another good fit.

Flush against a dark roof, a bifacial panel still works, and the back simply adds less.

## Bifacial is now common

Many new TOPCon panels are bifacial as standard. Of UTL's panels, the [630 Wp G12R](/products/630wp-g12r-topcon-bifacial-ndcr-solar-panel/), the [545 W](/products/545-watt-topcon-bifacial-solar-panel/) and the [520 W DCR](/products/520w-mono-dcr-bifacial-solar-panel/) are all bifacial. So on a new quote, the useful question is whether your mounting lets the back side contribute.

## Bifacial and the subsidy

For the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/), the panels must be DCR and on the ALMM list; being bifacial does not change that.
