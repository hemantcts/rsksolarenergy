Growatt is a large Chinese inverter maker, sold in India through distributors and installers. The warranty a buyer ends up with depends partly on what the seller registers, which is why the figure to trust is the one written on your own warranty card.

The two efficiency figures measure different things, so neither beats the other. MPPT efficiency is how closely the inverter follows the panels; maximum efficiency is how much power it converts at its best load.

## Choose Growatt if

- Your installer uses Growatt, has the monitoring set up, and will register an extended warranty in writing.
- You want the maximum efficiency figure stated outright, which UTL's page does not give in the same terms.

## Choose UTL if

- You want 10 years as the standard warranty, with nothing to apply for.
- You want the inverter, panels and a later battery from one brand and one service network, and a UTL on-grid inverter sized for your roof, from [3 kW](/3kw-solar-system-price-punjab/) to [5 kW](/5kw-solar-system-price-punjab/) and beyond.

## What to ask a Growatt seller

Ask which warranty they will register, standard or extended, and ask to see it in writing before you pay. Ask who in Punjab services the inverter if it fails. The same two questions are worth asking about any inverter, including ours.
