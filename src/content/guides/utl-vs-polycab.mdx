The two efficiency figures measure different things. MPPT efficiency is how closely the inverter follows the panels' best operating point; maximum efficiency is how much of the power it converts at its best load. A higher number on one does not beat a lower number on the other, so neither tells you on its own which inverter produces more. Our [solar calculator](/solar-calculator/) works from generation, which is what the bill sees.

## Choose UTL if

- You want 10 years of warranty without paying for an extension.
- You want the panels, the inverter and later a battery from one brand. UTL makes all three, and our [UTL inverter range](/products/inverters/) runs from home sizes to commercial ones.
- You are sizing a [3 kW](/3kw-solar-system-price-punjab/) or [5 kW](/5kw-solar-system-price-punjab/) home system and want it priced as one kit.

## Choose Polycab if

- You were going to pay for an extended warranty anyway, at which point both reach 10 years.
- You want the maximum efficiency figure stated outright, which UTL's page does not give in the same terms.
