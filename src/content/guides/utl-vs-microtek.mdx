On a small off-grid PCU it is a draw: Microtek lists a 2-year warranty on its Solar PCU 1235/12V, and UTL's Gamma units carry 2 years too. UTL pulls ahead on a grid-connected hybrid system, where its Sigma+ PCU lists 5 years, and its catalogue also runs to LiFePO4 lithium batteries and on-grid string inverters from 1.5 kW to 125 kW.

## Choose UTL if

- **You want a grid-connected hybrid system.** The Sigma+ PCU lists 5 years.
- **You want lithium batteries or a larger on-grid system** from the same brand as your panels and inverter.
- **You are buying through a local distributor** who stocks the full UTL range, such as RSK in Mohali.

## Choose Microtek if

- **You already use a Microtek inverter at home** and the model you want carries the warranty you need.
- **You find a Microtek model that fits your load better** than the UTL options we quote.
