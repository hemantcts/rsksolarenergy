## What your business pays PSPCL now

Shops, offices, clinics, schools and similar premises are billed under Non-Residential Supply (NRS). Energy is charged from the first unit, with no free allowance:

## Why solar suits business connections

- **Every unit is charged.** There is no free-units line, so every solar unit you use directly reduces the bill.
- **Daytime use.** Most businesses use power when the sun is up. Solar units used directly are worth the full tariff.
- **Predictable cost.** Tariffs change every year. A rooftop system fixes the cost of a large share of your power for 25 years.

What solar does not reduce: the fixed charges on your sanctioned load. Those stay the same.

## Sizing a commercial system

Start from twelve months of bills, not one, because business use varies by season. The system is sized to your daytime consumption and capped at your sanctioned load or contract demand. Larger systems are designed after a site survey: roof layout, shading, structure and the route for cabling to the meter all change the design.
