## What your business pays PSPCL now

Shops, offices, clinics, schools and similar premises are billed under Non-Residential Supply (NRS). Energy is charged from the first unit, with no free allowance:

## Why solar suits business connections

A business connection has no free-units line, so every solar unit you use comes straight off the bill. Most businesses also use their power while the sun is up, and units used on site are worth the full tariff. Tariffs change every year, while a rooftop system fixes the cost of a large share of your power for 25 years.

Solar won't reduce the fixed charges on your sanctioned load, though. Those stay the same.

## Sizing a commercial system

Start from twelve months of bills, because business use changes with the seasons. The system is sized to your daytime consumption and capped at your sanctioned load or contract demand. Larger systems are designed after a site survey, since the roof layout, shading, structure and the cable route to the meter all affect the design.

## Payback

Payback depends on the tariff, how much of the generation you use on site, and the system price. For a first estimate, enter a business bill in the [calculator](/solar-calculator/) and choose your connection type.
