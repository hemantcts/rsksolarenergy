A tenth of a point changes very little in practice. We plan on about 100 square feet of shade-free roof per kW, and a gap this small does not move that figure for a home system. Roof space, shading and price decide more.

## Choose Vikram Solar if

- You want the highest published efficiency of the two, and have the product warranty in writing at 12 years.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the panels to come with the inverter and battery from one brand, as a complete system.
- You need DCR panels for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/). UTL makes DCR modules, and our [solar panels guide](/solar-panels/) explains what DCR means.
