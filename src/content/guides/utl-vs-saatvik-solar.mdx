## Choose Saatvik if

- You want the highest efficiency and the longer product warranty of the two, on the panels alone.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the panels, inverter and battery from one brand with one service network.
- You need DCR panels for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/). Read [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/) before you compare prices, since the two are priced very differently.
