## Who it helps

- A home in a village or dhani where the line has not been laid, or where supply comes for only a few hours.
- A dera, farmhouse or tubewell room out in the fields, far from the nearest pole.
- A shop, school room or dispensary that needs lights and fans in the evening.
- A site where getting a new connection would take months or cost more than the system.

## What an off-grid inverter does

In an off-grid system, the panels charge a battery bank in the day, and the inverter turns battery power into normal 230 V AC for the house. In a solar PCU, the charge controller and inverter are in one box. Because there is no grid, nothing is exported and there is no electricity bill, but everything you use has to come from your own panels and batteries.

That makes it different from an inverter in a town, which charges its battery from the grid. An off-grid inverter's only source is the sun.

## What it can run

Start with the loads that matter most in the evening:

- LED lights, which use very little
- ceiling fans and a cooler
- a TV and a set-top box
- phone and torch charging
- a small fridge, on a larger system

Heavy loads such as an AC, a water heater, an iron or a large motor need a much larger inverter and battery bank, and they are where off-grid systems get expensive. Where you can, run them in the day, straight from the panels, or keep them off the solar system.

## Getting the size right

Most disappointment with off-grid solar comes from a system sized for a sunny summer day. Size it for the worst month instead. In Punjab that is December and January, when days are short and fog can cut output for several days running. Batteries also last longer when they are not run flat each night; we plan on using half of a tubular battery's capacity and 80% of a lithium battery's.

## Why it beats a generator or kerosene

Once it is in, sunlight costs nothing, and there is no fuel to buy, carry or store. It makes no noise and no fumes. The cost is up front, and the batteries need replacing during the life of the panels. [Off-grid solar or a diesel generator](/blog/off-grid-solar-vs-diesel-generator-punjab/) sets the costs side by side.

## Looking after it

The panels need cleaning when dust builds up. Tubular batteries need distilled water topped up from time to time; see [how to look after a tubular battery](/tubular-battery-care/). Lithium batteries need no water; see [how to look after a lithium battery](/lithium-battery-care/).
