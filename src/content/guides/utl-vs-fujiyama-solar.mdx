UTL Solar and Fujiyama Solar come from the same place. Fujiyama Power Systems Limited, based in Delhi, makes and sells rooftop solar products under both names, which is why the two ranges cover so much of the same ground: panels, solar inverters and PCUs, and batteries.

## Why the comparison still matters

Two products from one maker can still differ in warranty length, capacity, and whether a panel is DCR. That last one decides whether a system qualifies for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/), so it is worth more attention than the name on the box.

With one quote in each brand's name, check these, model against model:

- The warranty on the exact model, since it varies within each brand. UTL's 3.3 kW on-grid inverter, for example, lists 10 years.
- The capacity. A cheaper quote is sometimes a smaller system, and our [solar calculator](/solar-calculator/) gives the size your bill needs.
- The DCR certificate for the panels, if you want the subsidy.
- Who installs and services it, which depends on the dealer as much as on the brand.
