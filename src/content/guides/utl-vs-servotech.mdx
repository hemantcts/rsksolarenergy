Servotech is a Delhi company that makes EV chargers and inverters as well as TOPCon panels. On the panels, the efficiency is level with UTL's and the product warranty matches; the performance warranty is where they part.

A performance warranty covers output as the panel ages. Five extra years on it means five more years in which a panel making less than its promised share can be claimed.

## Choose Servotech if

- You already use Servotech equipment and want to stay with one supplier.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the longer performance warranty, 30 years against 25.
- You need DCR panels for the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/). See [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).
