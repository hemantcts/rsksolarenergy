Exide is best known for batteries, and on the tubular models compared here it lists longer warranties than UTL does. Where UTL comes back into it is the rest of the system: panels, the solar inverter or PCU, and the battery, all from one brand and one service network.

## Choose Exide if

- The battery warranty is what matters most to you. On these models, Exide lists longer free-replacement periods.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the battery, the inverter or PCU, and the panels from one brand, so one service network answers for the whole system. A [hybrid solar system](/hybrid-solar-systems/) is where that counts most.
- You are sizing the battery bank with the rest of the system. Our [hybrid calculator](/hybrid-solar-calculator/) works out the inverter, the batteries and the panels together.
