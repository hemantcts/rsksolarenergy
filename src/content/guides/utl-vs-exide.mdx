Exide is best known for batteries, and against UTL's 150 Ah tubulars it lists the longer warranties. UTL's 170 Ah USB 17100 lists a longer total than any of them. It is a larger battery, and UTL does not state how its 100 months divide between free replacement and pro-rata, so ask for that in writing before you weigh it.

## Choose Exide if

- The free-replacement period matters most. Exide states it outright: 54 months on the EL Ultra.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the battery, the inverter or PCU, and the panels from one brand, so one service network answers for the whole system. A [hybrid solar system](/hybrid-solar-systems/) is where that counts most.
- You are sizing the battery bank with the rest of the system. Our [hybrid calculator](/hybrid-solar-calculator/) works out the inverter, the batteries and the panels together.
