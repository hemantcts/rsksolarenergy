### This already runs

We supply shops and installers who sell UTL products under their own name, and we take on larger jobs alongside them where the work needs more hands than one business has. The arrangement below is not a new idea we are trying out on you.

That is the arrangement on offer here. You keep your own customers and your own name, and we are the stock behind you.

## What a UTL dealership gives you

You get the full UTL range at dealer pricing: solar panels, on-grid, hybrid and off-grid inverters and PCUs, batteries, charge controllers and complete system kits, all below the price we quote retail customers.

Behind that is a distributor that has supplied and installed UTL products from Phase 8B, Mohali since {BUSINESS.foundedYear} and deals with UTL directly on stock, specifications and warranty support.

You also get technical backup. If a customer asks about something outside what you stock day to day, ask us. Matching a panel, inverter and battery correctly is where installations most often go wrong.

## What we ask when you apply

We don't publish fixed minimum order values or an onboarding checklist, because dealer terms depend on the products and volumes involved, and we would sooner agree them with you directly. To get started, send us these on WhatsApp:

1. Your business name and city
2. What you currently sell or install
3. Your GST number, if you have one
4. Which UTL categories you want to stock: panels, inverters, batteries or complete kits

If you are an installer or electrician who only needs the occasional panel, inverter or battery, you don't need a dealer account; that is a trade enquiry. See [UTL Solar distributor](/utl-solar-distributor-punjab/) and send us the items and quantities on WhatsApp.
