## What a UTL dealership gives you

- **The full UTL range, at dealer pricing.** Solar panels, on-grid, hybrid and off-grid inverters and PCUs, batteries, charge controllers and complete system kits — lower than the price we quote a retail customer.
- **An established distributor behind you.** RSK has supplied and installed UTL products from Phase 8-B, Mohali since {BUSINESS.foundedYear}, so stock, specifications and warranty support come from a distributor who already deals directly with UTL.
- **Technical backup.** If a customer's query is beyond what you stock day to day, ask us — matching a panel, inverter and battery correctly is where installations go wrong.

## What we ask when you apply

We have not published fixed minimum order values or a formal onboarding checklist here — dealer terms depend on the product range and volume, and we would rather talk them through than post generic numbers that do not fit your business. To get started, send us on WhatsApp:

1. Your business name and city
2. What you currently sell or install
3. Your GST number, if you have one
4. Which UTL categories you want to stock — panels, inverters, batteries, or complete kits
