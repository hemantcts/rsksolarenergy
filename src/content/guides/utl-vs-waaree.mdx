Waaree's 700 Wp TOPCon bifacial module lists a 12-year product warranty and a 30-year power warranty at 22.32% efficiency. UTL's 545 W TOPCon bifacial panel lists a 30-year performance warranty at 22.74%, and its 630 Wp G12R TOPCon panel lists 23.32%. For a subsidised home system, the check that matters more than either figure is DCR: the Waaree module we compared is non-DCR, and UTL lists DCR TOPCon modules at 580 W and 625 W.

## Choose UTL if

- **You want DCR TOPCon panels for a subsidised home system** from the same brand as your inverter and battery.
- **Roof space is tight,** where the higher listed efficiency helps a little.

## Choose Waaree if

- **You need a 700 Wp class module** for a large commercial roof and the product warranty term matters most to you.
