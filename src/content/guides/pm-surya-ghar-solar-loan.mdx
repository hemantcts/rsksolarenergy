## What the scheme offers

Under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, public-sector banks lend for rooftop solar on terms the government has set out. From the Press Information Bureau's release of 11 March 2025:

<tr><th scope="row">Lenders</th><td>12 public-sector banks</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">Interest rate</th><td>Subsidised, 6.75%</td></tr>
<tr><th scope="row">Collateral</th><td>None, for loans up to ₹2 lakh</td></tr>

The rate is tied to wider interest rates and has moved before. Treat 6.75% as the published figure at that date and ask your bank for the current one.

## Who it suits

It is built for homes claiming the subsidy, and the subsidy covers home systems up to 3 kW. A ₹2 lakh collateral-free loan covers most of what a subsidised home system costs. Our [3 kW price page](/3kw-solar-system-price-punjab/) and the [solar calculator](/solar-calculator/) show the price before and after the subsidy for your size.

## How it fits with the subsidy

The loan and the subsidy are two separate flows of money:

1. The bank lends, and the system is paid for.
2. The system is installed and the DISCOM inspects it.
3. The subsidy is paid into your own bank account, typically 30 to 45 days after the inspection.
4. You repay the loan on the bank's schedule throughout.

The subsidy is never paid to the bank or to the installer, and it does not reduce the loan automatically. Plan your repayments as if it were a separate sum that arrives later, then decide whether to use it to pay the loan down early. Ask the bank whether early repayment carries a charge. For the subsidy itself, see [PM Surya Ghar in Punjab](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/).

## How to apply

Apply for the subsidy through the national portal, [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), where you also choose a registered vendor. The loan application is online too; ask your bank how it links to your subsidy application. Make sure the panels quoted are DCR and on the ALMM list, which the subsidy requires; see [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).

## If it does not suit you

For a larger system, a business, or a home the scheme does not cover, the other routes are in [five ways to finance solar](/solar-loan-options/): a home loan top-up, a personal loan, an NBFC, or our finance partner.
