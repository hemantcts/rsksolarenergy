## Choose Loom Solar if

- You want the longer product warranty, 15 years against UTL's 10.

## Choose UTL if

- You want the system quoted, installed and followed through the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/) by a local team.
- You need DCR panels for the subsidy. See [DCR and non-DCR panels](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).
