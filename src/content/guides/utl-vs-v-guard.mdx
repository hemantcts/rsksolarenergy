The two inverter efficiency figures measure different things and cannot be set against each other. MPPT efficiency is how closely the inverter follows the panels; maximum efficiency is how much power it converts at its best load.

## Choose V-Guard if

- The battery warranty matters most. The SPST15060 lists 60 months of free replacement.
