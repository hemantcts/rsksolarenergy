Start with the kind of system, because it decides the kind of inverter. An on-grid system uses a grid-tie string inverter and no batteries. A hybrid system uses a hybrid unit that works with both the grid and a battery bank. An off-grid system uses a solar inverter or PCU that runs from batteries alone. Capacity, battery voltage and phase come after that.

## Capacity

**On-grid:** match the inverter to the panels. A 3 kW array gets an inverter of about 3 kW, and UTL makes units at 3, 3.3 and 3.6 kW so there is room to fit the exact array.

**Hybrid and off-grid:** the inverter's kVA has to cover everything that runs at the same time during a power cut, plus the brief surge when a fridge, pump or cooler motor starts. Add up what you want to keep running and leave headroom above it. An inverter pushed to its limit every evening trips more often and ages faster.

## Battery voltage

The inverter's battery voltage has to match the bank: 12 V is one battery, 24 V is two, 48 V is four and 96 V is eight. UTL names its models this way, so a Sigma+ 2 kVA/48 V expects four 12 V batteries in series. Bigger systems use 48 V or 96 V because a higher voltage carries the same power at a lower current, with less loss in the cables.

If you want a lithium battery, check that the inverter supports lithium charging. UTL sells lithium-ready versions such as the GAMMA+ 3 kVA/24 V.

## Single phase or three phase

Match the inverter to your electricity connection. Most homes have a single-phase supply. Larger homes and most commercial buildings are three phase, which UTL's F3 on-grid models are built for.

## MPPT or PWM

An MPPT charge controller, built into UTL's Sigma+ and Gamma+ units, draws more power from the panels than a PWM controller, especially in heat and weak light. PWM costs less and is fine for a small lighting kit, but for a rooftop system MPPT is worth having.

## Warranty and service

UTL's kit sheets list 5 years on the Sigma inverters in its hybrid kits and 2 years on the Gamma 348 in its 3 kW off-grid kit. Inverter warranties run far shorter than the 25-year panel warranty, so it helps to buy through someone who can handle a claim locally.
