DCR tells you where the solar cells and modules are made. ALMM is the government’s approved list of models and manufacturers. If you want the PM Surya Ghar subsidy on an on-grid or hybrid system, your panels must be DCR and on ALMM.

That single rule decides the panel families we propose when you plan to apply for the subsidy. Without the subsidy, you can choose DCR or non-DCR. Either way, we write the exact make and model in your documents so the model you saw is the model we install.

## What DCR means, and what it does not

DCR stands for Domestic Content Requirement. It is an origin rule. A DCR panel uses cells made in India and is assembled in India. When a DCR panel is also on ALMM, it meets the subsidy’s panel condition.

DCR is not a performance label. Output and efficiency come from the technology and the specific model. If you are comparing technologies, start with our guides on [which panel technology is best in 2026](/best-solar-panel-technology/), [mono PERC and half-cut](/mono-perc-half-cut-solar-panels/) and [TOPCon](/topcon-solar-panels/). For model-level checks, see how to read a panel sheet in our [datasheet explainer](/blog/solar-panel-datasheet-what-matters/).

## What ALMM is, and where it matters

ALMM is the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers for PV modules. A model on ALMM has cleared the government’s approvals. For PM Surya Ghar on rooftops, on-grid and hybrid systems qualify only when the panels are DCR and on ALMM. Off-grid systems do not qualify for the subsidy, so ALMM and DCR do not change that.

When we design your subsidised system, we pick DCR models that are on ALMM and we write the exact model code in your proposal. At handover you can match the cartons and nameplates with your paperwork.

## When DCR and ALMM affect your choices

- You want the PM Surya Ghar subsidy: pick DCR panels that are on ALMM. The subsidy pays ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, capped at ₹78,000. It is paid into your own bank account 30 to 45 days after the DISCOM inspection. It never comes off the invoice, and it never goes to a lender or an installer. See the steps in our [Punjab guide](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/).
- You need backup: hybrid systems qualify when the panels meet DCR and ALMM. Off-grid does not. Compare types in our guide on [on-grid vs off-grid vs hybrid](/on-grid-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid/).
- You are not applying: you can choose DCR or non-DCR. Price and availability tend to decide, which we explain in [DCR vs non-DCR](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).

## Sizing and expected output still decide the value

Lists decide eligibility. Sizing decides how the system fits your bill and roof. We plan on about 135 units a month from each kW of panels in Punjab, averaged across the year. With TOPCon, output can reach up to about 180 units per kW in the sunniest months. In December and January fog, plan on about 75% of the average. All of these are estimates and depend on your roof, shade, dust and weather.

A quick way to see what size matters for your home is to compare your monthly units with what a size can make. The units you use as they are made are worth the most because they replace units you would have bought. Whatever is exported is settled under PSPCL’s rules, and our calculations give export no value at all. If you want to try sizes, use our [solar calculator](/solar-calculator/). For a new build, use the [new-house calculator](/new-house-solar-calculator/). If you need backup, the [hybrid calculator](/hybrid-solar-calculator/) helps you see how battery sizing interacts with daytime solar.

Two site rules matter in Punjab. Plan about 100 sq ft of shade-free roof for each kW. A rooftop system can be at most 100% of the sanctioned load. If you are near Punjab’s 300 free units a month line, read how size choices change here: [3 kW or 5 kW near the free-units line](/blog/3-kw-or-5-kw-when-you-sit-near-punjab-s-free-units-line/) and our 5 kW deep dive on [sizing and price](/blog/5-kw-solar-in-punjab-subsidy-price-and-right-sizing/).

For price planning, our installed on-grid estimates before subsidy are:
- 3 kW: ₹1,78,000 to ₹1,95,800
- 5 kW: ₹2,50,000 to ₹2,75,000

These are ranges. Hybrid always costs more than on-grid at the same size because of the battery and hybrid inverter. Off-grid kits are priced on a different basis and are not comparable size for size.

## What we write on your documents

DCR and ALMM only help if the installed panels match the model on paper. We keep it clear:

- Proposal and invoice show the brand and exact model.
- If you want, we add the model to the single-line diagram and hand over the datasheet.
- During installation you can cross check the carton labels and module nameplates against your paperwork.
