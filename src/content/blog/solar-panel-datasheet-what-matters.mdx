Every solar quote leads with a wattage: 400 W, 540 W and so on. It's the easiest figure to compare and, on its own, the least useful, especially on a Punjab roof where summer heat is the real test.

## Module efficiency: power from the same roof area

Module efficiency is the share of the sunlight hitting the panel that becomes electricity. A more efficient panel makes more power from the same roof area, which matters most when you are short of roof space. Two panels with the same wattage can differ in efficiency if they are different sizes, so check the dimensions next to the wattage.

## Temperature coefficient: the number that matters most in a Punjab summer

Panels are rated at a standard test condition of 25°C. On a Punjab rooftop in June they run far hotter, and output drops as panel temperature rises. The rate of that drop is the temperature coefficient, usually given as a percentage loss per degree above 25°C. A panel with a better (smaller) coefficient loses less output on the hottest days of the year, which is when a household's air-conditioning load is highest.

## ALMM listing decides your subsidy

The Approved List of Models and Manufacturers is the government's list of panel models eligible for PM Surya Ghar. It is a compliance requirement, and it isn't a measure of technical quality. A panel that performs just like a listed model but isn't on the list itself still fails inspection and loses the subsidy, and that can't be put right once the system is installed. Check the *exact model number* on your quote against the current ALMM list before you commit; the brand name alone isn't enough. For a subsidised home system the panel also has to be DCR, meaning built with Indian-made cells.

## Panel warranties come in two parts

Sales conversations often blur them together.

- The product warranty covers manufacturing defects in the panel itself, usually for a shorter period.
- The performance warranty promises the panel will still produce a stated share of its original rated output after a long period, commonly around 25 years. It often allows a bigger drop in the first year and a slower decline after that.

When a quote just says "25-year warranty", ask which one it means: cover against the panel failing, or a promise about how much power it will still produce.
