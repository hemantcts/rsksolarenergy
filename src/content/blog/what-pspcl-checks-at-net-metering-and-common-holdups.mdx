## What PSPCL looks for at inspection

For a smooth inspection, we make sure the application and the site line up on a few basics:

- Size cap: a rooftop system can be at most 100% of the sanctioned load.
- System type for subsidy: on-grid and hybrid qualify. Off-grid does not.
- Panels for subsidy: panels must be DCR and on the ALMM list when you claim PM Surya Ghar.

If you want to understand how credits work once the meter is live, see our post on [net metering in Punjab](/blog/net-metering-punjab-pspcl-explained/). The settlement details are covered in that guide.

## Why applications get held up

These are avoidable issues we see:

- Oversizing beyond sanctioned load. The cap is hard. Stay within 100% of sanctioned load.
- Non DCR or non ALMM panels on a subsidy file. PM Surya Ghar needs DCR and ALMM. If you have already bought modules, ask us to check the exact model before you submit.
- Applying for subsidy with an off-grid kit. Off-grid does not qualify. Only on-grid and hybrid qualify.
- Mismatch between what was applied and what was installed. Keep the make, model and size consistent across the form and the site.

The subsidy is paid into the applicant’s own bank account 30 to 45 days after the DISCOM inspection. It never comes off the invoice, and it never goes to a lender or an installer.

## Documents and details to line up before you book inspection

We keep these items consistent across the form and the site. It avoids last minute edits and repeat visits.

- Sanctioned load from your PSPCL bill. We size the inverter and array to stay within 100%.
- System type on the application and on the roof. On-grid or hybrid for subsidy.
- Exact panel make and model if subsidy is claimed. We verify DCR and ALMM status before we submit.
- Address on the bill and the installation address as photographed on site.

If you are building or renovating, plan panel layout, inverter wall space and cable routes early. Our guide on [planning a house for solar](/blog/building-a-house-plan-for-solar/) shows where to leave space so we do not have to change the design during the application.
