If your PSPCL bill is still high after installing solar, start with the net meter. Read import and export, then compare what you see to what your system should make in a month. The fix is usually in when the home uses power, the panels on the roof, or shade.

<Callout kind="problem">

"My 5 kW system went live, but my two month PSPCL bill is still bigger than I expected. The net meter shows export, the inverter app shows generation, and I am confused which number matters. Is something wrong with my installation?"

</Callout>

## What matters on the net meter

- Import is the power you still bought from the grid. That is what makes up your bill.
- Export is the surplus solar sent to the grid. It is settled under PSPCL's net metering rules, but in our own planning we give export no value at all.
- The biggest savings come from using solar as it is made in the day. Running heavy loads at night raises import even if your total generation is healthy.

For a walk through of the meter screens and where to find import and export on a PSPCL net meter, use our step by step guide: [how to read your solar meter and electricity bill](/read-solar-meter-and-electricity-bill/).

## Check your system’s expected monthly output

Across Punjab we plan on about 135 units a month from each kW of panels, averaged over the year. It is an estimate that depends on your roof, shade, dust and weather. In the sunniest months, systems with TOPCon panels can reach up to about 180 units per kW, and in December and January fog we plan on about 75% of the average.

## Step by step when a bill looks higher than it should

<Callout kind="fix">

- Note your system size in kW, then multiply by about 135 to get an estimated monthly generation. Treat it as a roof and weather dependent estimate.
- Read your net meter import and export for the bill period. Use our guide: [how to read your meter and bill](/read-solar-meter-and-electricity-bill/).
- Compare inverter app generation to the meter’s figures. Small differences happen. Look for big gaps only.
- Walk the roof at noon. Check for new shade from a tank, dish or a neighbour’s structure. Clean the glass if you see dust or smoke film.
- Shift daytime loads to sunny hours. Washing machine, geyser, ironing, and EV charging in the day cut import.
- If the issue is only in December and January, plan for lower output in fog season.
- If usage has grown or daytime use is low, consider upsizing within your sanctioned load limit or a hybrid option. Read [sanctioned load rules](/blog/sanctioned-load-and-rooftop-solar-size/) and see [system types](/on-grid-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid/).

</Callout>

## Why two similar homes get different bills from the same system

| With the same size system | Day use home | Night use home |
|---|---|---|
| When heavy loads run | Mostly in the day | Mostly after sunset |
| Net meter import | Lower, because solar serves loads directly | Higher, because grid serves night loads |
| Export reading | Often higher | Often lower |
| What to adjust first | Keep heavy appliances in sunny hours | Move some loads to the day or consider backup |
| If still short | Check shade or cleaning | Check shade or cleaning, then consider size within sanctioned load |

This is why a neighbour’s export number can be large while your bill is larger. The timing of use decides how much solar you keep for yourself.

## When is it a system problem

Most cases we see in Mohali and around are solved on the roof and in how power is used. Still, call us if you notice any of these:

- A clear drop on the inverter app after a storm or wiring change on site
- One string reading far lower than the other for days in similar sun
- Repeated inverter faults or tripping
- A sudden new shadow path across the array at many hours

We will test string by string, check connectors, and trace shade. If a product fault is found, the warranty is the manufacturer’s on their terms. RSK Solar Energy does not give a separate warranty.

If your roof allows, remember a rooftop system can be at most 100 percent of your sanctioned load. If you need more than that can allow, speak to us about sanctioned load changes and options.
