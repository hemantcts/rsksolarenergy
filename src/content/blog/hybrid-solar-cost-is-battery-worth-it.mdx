Hybrid solar gets asked about more than any other system type on this site, usually with one unspoken question underneath: is the battery actually worth what it adds to the price?

## The subsidy is not the deciding factor anymore

It used to be common advice that hybrid loses the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, making the battery's cost even harder to justify. That is not RSK's position: our hybrid systems are grid-tied and net-metered, not disconnected from PSPCL, so they receive the same subsidy as on-grid, up to ₹78,000 for a home. The subsidy question should not be what decides between the two; the battery's real value should be.

## What actually costs more, and why

The battery bank is the added cost over an equivalent on-grid system. It is not a fixed percentage markup; it scales with the battery capacity chosen, which depends on what you need to keep running during a cut and for how long. See our [real, itemised hybrid pricing](/hybrid-solar-systems/) for 3, 5 and 6 kW systems, taken from actual quotes rather than a rule-of-thumb multiplier.

## The question that actually decides it

Ask whether a power cut costs you something, not only whether you can afford the extra. A clinic that cannot lose power mid-procedure, a home office that loses a working day and a shop that stops trading all face real costs a battery protects against. An occasional, tolerable inconvenience is a different calculation: the same money might be better spent on a bigger on-grid system, or simply saved.

## Sizing the battery to the loads, not the whole house

A battery that could run air conditioners through a long cut would be very large and expensive. Most hybrid systems back up a chosen set of circuits, such as lights, fans, the fridge, the Wi-Fi and a computer, rather than the whole house. Before we quote, we ask what has to stay on during a cut and for how long, and size the battery to that answer specifically.

## An honest middle ground

If cuts are only occasional, an on-grid system with the full subsidy, plus a small separate inverter-battery for just the essentials, is often cheaper than a full hybrid system sized for everything. We will tell you which way the numbers fall for your situation rather than push the more expensive option by default.
