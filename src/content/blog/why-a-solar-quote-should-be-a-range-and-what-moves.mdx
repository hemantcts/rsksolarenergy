A solar quote should be a range because real roofs and real choices move the cost. Here is what shifts a Punjab rooftop quote up or down, and what you can check before you ask us.

<Callout kind="problem">

"I got two quotes for 5 kW. One is far cheaper. Which one is right, and what will the final bill be after subsidy and net metering?" That is what we hear most. The worry is paying more than you should, or buying a system that is not sized or specced right for your home.

</Callout>

## Why quotes start as a range

A rooftop system has parts you see, and parts you do not. Both change with your site.

- Size drives almost everything. We plan about 135 units a month per kW in Punjab across the year. It is an estimate and depends on the roof, shade, dust and weather.
- Roof access and structure decide mounting and labour. A flat slab with room is one job. A tight slope with parapets, longer cable runs and no parking for a day is another.
- Type matters. Hybrid with a battery always costs more than on-grid at the same size because of the battery and the hybrid inverter. Off-grid is priced on a different basis and is not comparable size for size.
- Parts choices vary. DCR and ALMM panels are required for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Panel technology and inverter brand options are in our ranges and on our [panels](/products/solar-panels/) and [inverters](/products/inverters/) pages.
- Paperwork and limits set the cap. Your rooftop size and your sanctioned load both cap system size. A rooftop system can be at most 100 percent of the sanctioned load.

Our installed price estimates before subsidy are ranges by design: 3 kW on-grid ₹1,78,000 to ₹1,95,800, and 5 kW on-grid ₹2,50,000 to ₹2,75,000. Every price on our site is an estimate until the site is checked.

## What moves a quote up or down

Here is what we look at on a visit, and why it changes the number you see on paper:

- Roof and structure
  - Clear, shade-free area: plan about 100 sq ft per kW. If panels need a higher structure to clear shade, steel goes up.
  - Access: easy lift or narrow stair. Extra handling changes labour.
  - Cable distance from array to inverter to meter. Longer runs need more cable and conduit.
- System type and size
  - On-grid or hybrid. Hybrid adds the battery and a hybrid inverter, so it prices higher at every size. See our [on-grid vs off-grid vs hybrid guide](/on-grid-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid/).
  - Size you actually need. If your home sits near Punjab’s 300 free units a month, there may be nothing to save at small sizes. Read our note on this in [3 kW or 5 kW near the free-units line](/blog/3-kw-or-5-kw-when-you-sit-near-punjab-s-free-units-line/).
- Panels and compliance
  - DCR and ALMM-listed panels are required for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. See [DCR vs non-DCR](/dcr-vs-non-dcr-solar-panels/).
  - Panel wattage and technology are options, and we size to your roof. For technology basics see [mono PERC and half-cut](/mono-perc-half-cut-solar-panels/) and [TOPCon](/topcon-solar-panels/).
- Paperwork and metering
  - Sanctioned load caps system size. If you need a bigger system than your cap allows, we will point you to the guide and next steps in [sanctioned load and rooftop solar size](/blog/sanctioned-load-and-rooftop-solar-size/).
  - Net metering is under PSPCL’s rules. Units you use as they are made are worth most. We give export no value in our savings calculations.

## The subsidy and why the invoice does not change

If you claim PM Surya Ghar, the amounts are ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, capped at ₹78,000. The subsidy is paid into your own bank account 30 to 45 days after the DISCOM inspection. It never comes off the invoice, and it never goes to a lender or an installer. On-grid and hybrid qualify. Off-grid does not. Panels must be DCR and on the ALMM list. For the steps, use our [Punjab subsidy guide](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-punjab/).

All three are linked. Plan about 135 units a month per kW in Punjab across the year. With TOPCon panels, output can reach up to about 180 units per kW in the sunniest months. In December and January fog, plan on about 75 percent of the average. Every figure is an estimate and depends on the roof, shade, dust and weather. ## Why one quote looks much cheaper

When a quote looks far cheaper, it is usually because one or more of these are missing or different:

- System type is on-grid in one quote and hybrid in the other.
- DCR and ALMM-listed panels are quoted in one, non-compliant in the other. If you claim subsidy, compliance is required.
- Structure height and thickness are assumed as standard, but your roof needs more to clear shade.
- Cable lengths, DP box, earthing, protections and ACDB/DCDB are not itemized the same way.
- The size exceeds your sanctioned load or your roof. That size will not pass.

Ask us to line-item the BOS, structure spec and cable runs. We are happy to match line for line. For background on parts, see [what decides a panel’s price in Punjab](/blog/what-decides-solar-panel-price-punjab/) and [how to choose a solar inverter](/blog/how-to-choose-solar-inverter-for-home/).
